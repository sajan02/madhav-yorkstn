SOLUTIONS
What We Do

The Problems
We Solve for You

India's premium retail market is full of opportunity — and full of friction. Yorkstn removes the friction so brands stay focused on what they do best.

Why brands struggle
in India — and how we fix it

Every problem below is real and recurring. We've built Yorkstn's model around solving each one before they become your problem.

01

Regulatory Complexity

BIS certifications, CAROTAR customs rules, GST registration, DPIIT approvals — India's compliance stack is layered and unforgiving for foreign brands.

We manage the complete regulatory roadmap before your first shipment

02

Wrong Retail Location

Premium real estate in Delhi NCR requires local relationships and negotiation fluency. International brands often overpay or sign unfavorable lease terms.

We identify, evaluate, and negotiate retail spaces aligned to your brand tier

03

Store Identity Gets Lost

Indian buildout vendors rarely understand premium brand aesthetics. Material compromises misrepresent your brand at its most critical moment.

We project-manage every detail of store setup against your brand guidelines

04

No Ground-Level Intelligence

Generic market reports can't answer whether your specific category and price point will resonate in Gurugram's premium consumer segment.

We deliver a tailored India feasibility report specific to your brand

05

Supply Chain Complexity

Freight forwarding, customs clearance, GST-compliant invoicing, e-way bills — without a local expert this leads to stockouts, holds, and margin erosion.

We operate your India-side supply chain end-to-end

06

Speed Dilutes Premiumness

Scaling too fast — multiple cities, wide SKU range, over-licensing — is the most common reason once-aspirational brands become commonplace within years.

We build community first; geography follows once the foundation is real

Our Services

Six things we do.
All of them completely.

Before a product is shipped, brands need ground-truth intelligence. We produce a tailored feasibility report covering consumer segment analysis, competitive landscape, pricing feasibility, and a recommended phased entry roadmap — built for your specific category and brand tier in Delhi NCR.

What you receive

  • Consumer segment & spending profile for Delhi NCR
  • Competitive landscape & category white-space
  • Price positioning & import duty modelling
  • Recommended phased entry roadmap
  • Retail channel strategy (EBO vs. MBO)

We handle the complete regulatory stack — entity incorporation, DPIIT approvals, BIS product certifications, CAROTAR customs documentation, import licensing, and GST registration. All of it in place before your first product enters India.

What you receive

  • Indian entity incorporation (Pvt Ltd or LLP)
  • DPIIT single-brand retail FDI approval
  • BIS mandatory product certification
  • CAROTAR rules-of-origin documentation
  • GST registration & ongoing compliance
  • Import Export Code (IEC)

We leverage our established retail network in Gurugram — including our association with Value Homz — to identify spaces that align with your brand positioning. We evaluate each location, negotiate lease terms, and ensure brand standards are protected in the agreement.

What you receive

  • Shortlisted locations with scored comparison
  • Footfall & customer profile per site
  • Lease term negotiation & review
  • Brand standards written into lease
  • Mall / developer relationship management

We project-manage the entire store fit-out against your brand guidelines — sourcing vendors who can meet specification, overseeing materials and fixtures, managing lighting design, and ensuring every element from signage to visual merchandising reflects your identity precisely.

What you receive

  • Visual merchandising & layout
  • Soft launch event management
  • Post-launch brand compliance audits

We operate the full India-side supply chain: freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, replenishment planning, and e-way bill compliance. From your factory in Japan to the shelf in Gurugram — managed without you needing to intervene.

What you receive

  • Freight forwarding & customs clearance
  • Warehouse & storage management
  • GST-compliant invoicing & e-way bills
  • Inventory replenishment planning
  • Monthly inventory & sell-through reporting

Commerce follows community. We identify early adopters and tastemakers in Delhi NCR, organise curated preview events, and deliberately seed the brand into the right lifestyle communities — building genuine loyalty before broad marketing spend begins.

INDIA
India Market

The Opportunity
is Right Now

India ranks third globally in purchasing power parity and its premium consumer base is shifting — toward quality, provenance, and long-term value. Delhi NCR is where this shift is most concentrated.

Why India, Why Now

India doesn't need
more brands.
It needs the right ones.

The Indian consumer right for a Japanese craft brand is well-travelled, globally informed, and actively seeking products with genuine provenance and long-term value. They've experienced these brands abroad. They're waiting for an authentic, well-presented access point at home.

The challenge isn't demand — it's the absence of a disciplined entry partner who protects brand identity while navigating India's structural complexity. That gap is exactly what Yorkstn fills.

Why Japan Specifically

A shared philosophy
of craft & patience

Japan's brand-building philosophy — discipline, patience, and respect for craft — closely mirrors how we see the right way to enter India. Community comes before commerce. The product comes before scale.

This connects deeply with India's own cultural emphasis on relationships and long-term trust. We understand markets where reputation matters more than advertising reach.

I

Philosophical Alignment

Japanese brands build for longevity. So does India's premium consumer. The fit is cultural, not just commercial.

II

Category White Space

Japanese craft categories — ceramics, stationery, lifestyle goods, fashion — have very limited authentic representation in India's premium retail.

III

First-Mover Advantage

Brands that establish presence now will define what Japanese premium means to the Indian consumer for years ahead.
